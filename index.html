About us

A grassroots initiative closing the digital divide, one classroom at a time.

Build & Bloom Africa is a volunteer-powered programme working to close the digital divide for children living in underserved communities in informal settlements. We began in Mathare, Nairobi, with one shared laptop in a borrowed room, and have grown into a standing weekly class, built and sustained by the community it serves.

We work with children in Grades 4–9, many of whom had never had the chance to touch a computer before joining us. Our approach is simple: meet children where they are, teach them practical digital skills, and let them carry that knowledge forward to their peers.

Mission & vision

What we're working toward.

Our mission

Bring digital literacy and AI education to every child, regardless of where they grow up.

We equip children in underserved communities with practical digital skills, hands-on AI exposure, and the confidence to see technology as a path open to them.

Our vision

A Tech and AI Innovation Centre for Mathare.

Turning curious children into confident coders, designers, and founders, raising a generation of African innovators who build for the continent.

What we do

Four pillars of the programme.

Clear, practical blocks that define every week at Build & Bloom Africa.

💻

Digital Literacy & AI Basics

From first-time computer use to a full curriculum including Microsoft Word, children build practical, transferable digital skills.

🚀

Careers in Tech

A dedicated lesson introduces children to the range of careers the tech and data world can offer, planting the idea early that this path is open to them.

🌸

Gender Inclusion

We started with a single girl in the room. Through direct conversations with parents, girls now make up 70% of our class attendance.

🥣

Porridge Programme

A simple porridge programme runs alongside our lessons, so hunger is not what stands between a child and an hour of learning.

Learner journey

How children progress through the programme.

A simple path from first touch to peer leadership.

1

First computer use

Many children have never touched a keyboard before. We start with the basics: mouse, keyboard, and navigating a screen.

2

Core digital skills

Children build confidence with everyday tools and structured lessons around shared laptops in small groups.

3

Microsoft Word & projects

A dedicated Word session and Canva design projects, ending with children presenting their own work to classmates.

4

AI exposure

Hands-on introduction to AI concepts, showing children that technology is something they can understand and use.

5

Careers awareness

A careers-in-tech lesson plants the idea that data science, design, and coding are paths open to them.

6

Peer learning

Children who grasp a skill first pass it on to classmates, the same philosophy the programme itself was built on.
