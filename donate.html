Every contribution keeps a free session running.

Build & Bloom Africa runs on community support. Before you donate, here is exactly what your gift helps us provide for children in Mathare.

What your support enables

We use verified programme needs only. Funds go directly toward keeping weekly classes free, practical, and accessible.

Refurbished laptops

The programme was built on one shared laptop. Additional devices mean more children can practice hands-on each session instead of waiting for a turn.

Learning materials

Printed guides, notebooks, and session supplies so children can follow along and take skills home to their peers.

Porridge programme

A simple meal before or between lessons, so hunger is not what stands between a child and an hour of learning.

Venue & transport

Partner space at WhyNot Academy and volunteer travel costs to show up consistently, every week, in Mathare.

Internet & data

Connectivity for online tools, Canva projects, careers-in-tech exposure, and the digital skills curriculum children are building.

Programme growth

Scaling from a borrowed room to a standing weekly class, and toward a future Tech and AI Innovation Centre for the community.
