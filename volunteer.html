Volunteer

Show up with a skill, a laptop, or an hour.

Our programme runs on volunteers who believe every child deserves access to technology. Here is how to get started.

Your volunteer pathway

Whether you can teach, assist, or support behind the scenes, we would like to hear from you.

1
Fill the interest form

Share your name, skills, availability, and how you would like to help: teaching, assisting, equipment, or other support.

2
We follow up

Our team responds by email or WhatsApp to discuss fit, session times, and next steps.

3
Join a session

Volunteers rotate into small teaching teams in Mathare, keeping class sizes workable as attendance grows.

Prefer WhatsApp or email? Send us a message with your skills, availability, and area of interest.
