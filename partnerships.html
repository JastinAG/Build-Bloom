Partnerships

Work with us to close the digital divide.

Build & Bloom Africa welcomes schools, companies, funders, NGOs, and community organisations that want to support practical, free digital education in Mathare.

Schools & learning centres

  • Host or co-host sessions at your venue
  • Refer learners in Grades 4–9
  • Align curriculum with digital literacy goals
  • Support safeguarding and child protection standards

Companies & tech organisations

  • Donate refurbished laptops or equipment
  • Provide employee volunteers for teaching
  • Sponsor internet, data, or learning materials
  • Offer careers-in-tech exposure sessions

Funders & donors

  • Fund the porridge programme or session supplies
  • Support venue and transport costs
  • Back programme scale-up and equipment needs
  • Contribute via M-Changa or direct partnership

NGOs & media

  • Collaborate on community outreach in Mathare
  • Support gender inclusion and parent engagement
  • Cover youth-led STEM initiatives in Africa
  • Amplify learner and volunteer stories (with consent)
