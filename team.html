Meet the team

The volunteers who show up every week.

Friends, mentors, and young professionals lending an hour, a laptop, or a lesson plan of their own, so children in Mathare get a real shot at digital skills.

Cantwel Njeri Otieno

Founder & Lead Mentor

Data science mentor and the person who started it all: one room, one laptop, and a conviction that every child deserves access to technology. Cantwel still leads sessions, builds curriculum, and coordinates the volunteer team.
